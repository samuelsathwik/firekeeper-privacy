## 1. What Firekeeper is

Firekeeper helps you build a habit of reading the Bible: a reading plan, reminders at the times
you choose (called gates), a short fill-in-the-blank check after you read, a streak, and a
journal for your own reflections.

- **The app adds no commentary.** The Bible text is shown as published. Your reflections are
  your own words. Nothing in the app is a church, a pastor or professional advice of any kind.
- **Reminders are best effort.** Gates depend on your phone's settings, battery saving and
  Android itself. We work hard to make them arrive on time, but we can't promise every gate will
  appear. Quiet hours are always respected: no gate appears during them.
- **Streaks are a tool, not a judgement.** Grace days and freezes exist to be merciful. A streak
  has no monetary value.
