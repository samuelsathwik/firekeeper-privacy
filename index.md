# Firekeeper Privacy Policy

_Effective 12 November 2026_

Firekeeper ("the app") is a Bible-reading habit app published by Noa Technologies. This policy
explains what happens to your information when you use it. Our
[Terms of Use](https://samuelsathwik.github.io/firekeeper-privacy/terms.html) apply too.

## The short version
Your reading stays on your phone. Your plan, progress, streak, highlights, notes, bookmarks and
reflections are never sent to us. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we
never sell your information.

Two things use the internet:
- **Subscriptions** go through Google Play, with RevenueCat checking whether your subscription
  is active.
- **The community** (optional, in some versions of the app): if you choose to join, your public
  name and what you post are stored on our community server so others can see them.

## What the app stores on your phone
- Your reading plan, chosen reading times and quiet hours
- Your reading progress, streak and the history of why it changed
- Your highlights, notes and bookmarks
- The reflections you write in your journal
- Your settings, such as translation, text size and sounds

This information is stored only on your device. We never receive it. It is removed when you
uninstall the app or clear its data. Android's own device backup may include it, under your
Google account's backup settings.

## Subscriptions
Purchases are made through Google Play; we never see your payment details. To check whether
your subscription is active on your devices, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives your
purchase records from Google Play together with a random identifier created by the app (not
your name or email). RevenueCat processes this on our behalf under its own
[privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## Your community account (optional)
Joining the community is optional, and the rest of the app works without it. If you join, you
sign in with your Google account and choose a name others will see.

**What we store:** your Google account id, the name you chose, the date you joined, your posts,
comments and likes, the reports you make, and your private lists of hidden posts and blocked
members. The app doesn't store your email address; Google sign-in shares it with Google
Firebase Authentication, which signs you in.

**Why:** to run the community: showing discussions and likes, remembering your hidden and
blocked lists, and reviewing reports.

**Who can see it:** anyone using the community can see your public name, your published posts
and comments, and your likes. Your reports, your hidden and blocked lists, and posts waiting
for review are not shown to other members.

**Where:** with Google Firebase, in the United States. Your reading, plans, streak, highlights,
notes and reflections are never stored there.

## Community moderation
Posts and comments are checked against the community rules before others see them. Today these
checks run in the app. Before the community opens to the public, the same checks will also run
on our server, and posts the rules can't decide may be sent to an AI model (Anthropic's Claude)
for a second check. That AI check is not active yet; we'll update this policy before it starts,
including what is shared and how long it is kept. It will only ever see community posts, never
your private reading, notes or reflections. Reports about content that endangers children are
passed to the authorities as the law requires.

## Deleting your community account
In the app, open **Settings → Delete my community account**. This removes your account, your
posts, comments and likes, your reports and your hidden and blocked lists at once, and deletes
your sign-in record. Your reading data on your phone stays in place.

Without the app, email **lampuntomyfeet0777@gmail.com** from the Google account you used and ask
us to delete your community account. We'll delete everything within 30 days. Reports that other
members made about your posts may be kept for up to 90 days for safety review, then deleted.

## Permissions
- **Notifications and full-screen reminders:** to show your reading reminder at the times you
  choose, including over the lock screen.
- **Exact alarms:** so reminders arrive at the exact minute you chose.
- **Run at startup:** to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.
- **Internet and network state:** for subscriptions (Google Play and RevenueCat) and, if you
  join, the community.

The app does not use your advertising ID, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

## Children
Firekeeper is not directed at children under 13, and children under 13 may not join the
community.
